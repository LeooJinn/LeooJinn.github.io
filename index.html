A PPO agent, written from scratch in PyTorch, that learns to land a simulated rocket, with held-out evaluation and an honest account of what made training stable.

  • PPOfrom scratch in PyTorch
  • 19 of 20seeds land 90%+ of unseen starts
  • 500held-out landings per seed

A NumPy simulator behind the Gymnasium API, a PPO agent trained from scratch, and an evaluation protocol that checks test seeds never overlap training. Diagnosing why some seeds failed (policies running out of fuel before touchdown) led to a fuel-aware reward: 19 of 20 training seeds now land at least 90% of 500 unseen starts, against 12 of 20 with the original reward. A few seeds are still imperfect, and the repository documents them.

  • Python
  • PyTorch
  • Gymnasium
  • NumPy
